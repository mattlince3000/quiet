Privacy Policy for Quiet

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Quiet collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server. The app has no networking code of any kind.

What Quiet does with your messages

When iOS receives a text from a number that is not in your contacts, it may pass the message text and the sender's number to Quiet's message filter extension. Quiet compares that text against a fixed list of patterns stored inside the app and returns a single decision to iOS: deliver the message normally, or place it in the Junk folder.

  • Message text exists only in memory, for the fraction of a second that decision takes.
  • Message text and sender numbers are never written to disk, never logged, and never transmitted.
  • Quiet cannot read messages from people in your contacts. iOS does not send them to filters.
  • Quiet has no access to your contacts, photos, location, or any other personal data.

What Quiet stores on your device

Quiet keeps a small amount of data in a shared container on your iPhone, so the app and its filter extension agree on your settings. It holds only:

  • Your sensitivity choice (Standard or Aggressive)
  • Whether "Always allow codes and alerts" is on
  • A count of messages filtered this week, and in total
  • The date the filter last ran

No message content or sender information is included. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Test Lab

The Test Lab screen lets you paste a message to see how Quiet would treat it. Text you paste there is not saved and not sent anywhere. It is discarded when you leave the screen.

Children

Quiet does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
